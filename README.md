### Why Does This Even Exist?
Let's address the elephant in the room: the internet has turned ancient Indian texts into an endless factory of motivational hustle-porn. 

No, Lord Krishna never said *"Wake up at 5:00 AM, grind in silence, and cut off your fake friends."* That was a crypto influencer on LinkedIn. Yet somehow, half of social media slaps a flute track behind a random quote and calls it the *Bhagavad Gita*.

**PARAMPARA** is an interactive web platform designed to solve this mess. We are not building another generic fact directory or a glorified bhakti chatbot. We are building a public digital filter that tracks how an idea travels across time:

```text
PRIMARY SOURCE  ⟶  INTERPRETATION  ⟶  TRADITION  ⟶  INTERNET CLAIM

```
The golden rule here is: **Source ≠ Interpretation ≠ Tradition ≠ Internet Claim.**
### What's Inside the App (The Sneak Peek)
 * **Verify a Claim:** Enter a viral quote. The site runs it against verified texts and delivers the verdict: *Authentic*, *Context-Stripped*, or *Straight-up WhatsApp Fiction*.
 * **Trace an Idea:** An interactive journey showing how concepts like *Dharma*, *Karma Yoga*, and *Detachment* went from primary texts into classical commentaries, folk evolutions, and modern hot-takes.
 * **Explore Parampara:** An interactive cultural map showing how Krishna traditions and Janmashtami actually look across different states (food, folk music, regional arts) without flattening India into a monoculture.
 * **The IKS Decision Lab:** A dilemma solver connecting everyday chaos (burnout, decision paralysis, imposter syndrome) with philosophical frameworks—labeled strictly as modern reflections, not ancient life hacks.
 * **The Knowledge Compass:** Clear visual badges across every screen:
   * 📖 PRIMARY SOURCE (Straight out of the text)
   * 🧠 INTERPRETATION (Scholarly/commentator view)
   * 🪷 TRADITION (Lived cultural/regional practice)
   * 🌐 MODERN APPLICATION (Contemporary food-for-thought)
   * ⚠️ UNVERIFIED CLAIM (Needs serious fact-checking)
### Pick Your Fighter (How to Contribute)
You do not need a PhD in Sanskrit or a staff engineer badge to help. We need different brains for different parts of this machine:
#### 1. The Researchers & Builders
You dive into texts, outline concepts, write markdown briefs, or integrate them into our frontend database.
 * Check /research-papers for existing work.
 * Submitting a new topic? Drop a clean breakdown named: TopicName_YourName.md.
 * Put your real name or GitHub handle on it. Take credit for your homework.
#### 2. The Verifiers (The Myth-Busters)
Found a paper or site entry where someone cited an unverified verse or hallucinated context? Call it out and fix it.
 * Don't silently delete someone's hard work.
 * Submit a correction paper named: Fixing_OriginalPaperName.md.
 * Explain the factual error, cite the legitimate primary source, and update the site data.
#### 3. Regional Culture Keepers
Our map is only as rich as the people contributing to it. If your home region has an authentic folk song, a community ritual, or a local dish tied to these traditions that never gets covered in mainstream media, document it and help us map it out accurately.
#### 4. Code & UI Mechanics
 * Fix responsive layouts breaking on mobile screens.
 * Optimize our JSON structure and search filters.
 * Make animations snappy instead of laggy.
 * If you know how to center a div without crashing the entire DOM, you are automatically our hero.
### The Ground Rules on AI 🤖
Let's keep it real: **Using AI is fine, but blind copy-pasting is forbidden.**
I used AI to help brainstorm and speed up parts of this project, and you can too. Use LLMs to structure thoughts, summarize dense passages, or spot angles you missed.
**However:** LLMs are notorious for hallucinating verse numbers and inventing quotes with total confidence.
 * If your research paper or PR claims a verse exists, **you must manually verify the primary text.**
 * Every entry must cite the book, chapter, verse, or verified cultural history.
 * If your contribution reads like raw ChatGPT generated it without human review, it will be closed without debate.
