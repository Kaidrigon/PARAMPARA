# 2. Initial Observation: IKS Is Vast

Indian Knowledge Systems covers a very large and diverse body of knowledge and traditions.

During the initial exploration, it became clear that simply choosing “IKS” as a topic does not automatically produce a useful product. A beginner can encounter information about philosophy, literature, science, mathematics, medicine, arts, architecture, culture, traditions and many other areas.

### Initial thought

> “IKS is huge. Where does a person who knows nothing about it even start?”

This led to the first research question:

### Research Question 01

**How is IKS currently presented, preserved, researched and communicated digitally, and what would a beginner need in order to navigate it?**

---

# 3. Observation: Information Exists, But Finding a Clear Entry Point Is Difficult

During the initial research, I found that information about IKS exists across many different sources and formats.

There are academic materials, institutional resources, articles, videos, presentations, cultural resources and other forms of information.

However, I found it difficult to identify a single beginner-friendly platform that works as a clear starting point for someone with little or no prior knowledge of IKS.

This distinction is important.

I am **not** claiming that no useful IKS platforms exist.

The observation is:

> **There is substantial information available, but a beginner may not have an obvious, structured path through it.**

### Product implication

Instead of creating another static collection of information, PARAMPARA could function as a **digital gateway**.

A possible journey would be:

```text
WHAT IS IKS?
      ↓
WHAT ARE ITS DIFFERENT AREAS?
      ↓
WHAT INTERESTS ME?
      ↓
WHERE DOES THIS IDEA COME FROM?
      ↓
WHAT IS ITS ORIGINAL CONTEXT?
      ↓
HOW HAS IT BEEN INTERPRETED?
      ↓
HOW CAN IT BE UNDERSTOOD TODAY?
```

This became one of the foundations of PARAMPARA.

# 4. Observation: The “Glue” Problem

While researching applications of the Bhagavad Gita, I encountered attempts to connect its ideas with modern fields such as management and leadership education.

This raised a more interesting question.

Modern applications often have to **connect** an ancient text or tradition with a modern subject.

I started informally thinking of this as the:

> ## “Glue Problem”

The metaphor is simple:

**Modern problem + ancient concept = glue them together**

But is that enough?

For example, a person is not only a category such as:

- MBA student
- college student
- employee
- professional

An individual may simultaneously experience:

- academic pressure
- friendship problems
- family circumstances
- uncertainty
- conflict
- difficult decisions
- disappointment
- expectations about the future

This led to the hypothesis that a meaningful modern application may need to begin with the **person's actual situation**, rather than only with a broad demographic or academic category.

### Research Question 02

**Can IKS concepts be presented in relation to individual situations while still preserving the distinction between the original source and a modern interpretation?**

---

# 5. Observation: Modernization Is Not Just Visual

Another observation emerged from looking at some existing educational material.

A resource can contain valuable information while still feeling like an old presentation format.

For example, some material encountered during exploration appeared primarily as slides, images or presentation-style educational content.

This raised a distinction:

> **Modernization is not simply putting old information on a website.**

A modern digital experience can change:

- how information is discovered
- how concepts are connected
- how users interact with the material
- how context is displayed
- how sources are shown
- how a user moves from curiosity to understanding

Therefore, PARAMPARA should investigate **experience design**, not merely visual redesign.

# 6. Observation: Personalization Creates an Accuracy Problem

AI introduces another interesting possibility.

But jokes aside a modern AI system can take an individual's situation and produce a response that feels highly relevant to them.

For example:

```text
PERSONAL SITUATION
        ↓
AI RESPONSE
        ↓
“THIS FEELS RELEVANT TO ME”
```

But relevance does not automatically mean accuracy.

When dealing with historical, philosophical or religious texts, an AI-generated response could potentially:

- simplify an idea
- paraphrase it
- combine different interpretations
- misattribute a statement
- remove the original context
- present a modern interpretation as though it were the original teaching

This creates a central question for PARAMPARA:

> **Can something be personalized without pretending that the original source said something it did not?**

This became one of the reasons for the project's emphasis on source and context.

# 7. Emerging Principle: Source Is Not Application

The observations above led to a initial design principle:

```text
PRIMARY SOURCE
      ↓
ORIGINAL CONTEXT
      ↓
INTERPRETATION
      ↓
TRADITION / CULTURAL DEVELOPMENT
      ↓
MODERN APPLICATION
```

These layers should not automatically be presented as the same thing.

For example:

> **“The text says X”**

is different from:

> **“A later commentator interpreted X as Y”**

which is different from:

> **“This tradition developed around X”**

which is different again from:

> **“X can be thoughtfully discussed in relation to a modern situation.”**

This distinction is currently one of the central ideas behind PARAMPARA.

```text
 ↓

 ↓
```

The exact methodology for verification will need to be researched carefully before implementation.

## 8.5 IKS Decision Lab

A possible interactive feature where a user begins with a real-world situation rather than being given a predetermined philosophical answer.

Possible structure:

```text

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
```

The system should not imply:

Instead, it should help the user understand the relevant concept and distinguish source material from modern reflection.

# 10. Research Questions To Continue

The next stage of research should investigate:

### IKS as a field
- What exactly is included within the IKS framework?
- How is IKS defined by official and academic sources?
- What major knowledge domains are represented?

### Digital accessibility
- What digital IKS platforms already exist?
- Who are they designed for?
- How do they organize information?
- What works well?
- Where might beginners struggle?

### Bhagavad Gita
- What are reliable primary/textual sources?
- How is the Gita structured?
- Which concepts are most relevant to the proposed experience?
- How do different interpretations differ?

### Modern application
- How have scholars and educators applied Gita concepts to modern life?
- What are the strengths and weaknesses of these approaches?
- What happens when modern application becomes detached from textual context?

### AI and accuracy
- What are the risks of AI-generated interpretations of historical/philosophical texts?
- How should a digital product distinguish source material from generated or modern interpretation?
- Can AI be used as an interface without making it the authority?

### Cultural diversity
- How do Krishna and Janmashtami traditions vary across regions?
- How should different traditions be represented without treating one as the only “correct” version?

# 11. Research Standard

PARAMPARA should follow a simple rule:

> **Do not make a claim merely because it sounds plausible. Find out where it comes from.**

Whenever possible, research notes should record:

```text
CLAIM
SOURCE
SOURCE TYPE
CONTEXT
WHAT THE SOURCE ACTUALLY SUPPORTS
INTERPRETATION
CONFIDENCE / UNCERTAINTY
DESIGN DECISION
```

Primary sources, institutional sources and credible academic material should be preferred where appropriate.

If something is uncertain, the project should be comfortable saying:

> **“This is uncertain.”**

Accuracy is more valuable than sounding authoritative.

# 13. Current Hypothesis

At this stage, the working hypothesis is:

> **Indian Knowledge Systems contains a vast amount of knowledge, but a beginner may struggle to find a clear and contextual pathway through it. Existing attempts to connect traditional knowledge with modern life can also risk becoming broad, static or detached from individual context. PARAMPARA therefore explores a digital experience where users can discover IKS through structured exploration while keeping source, context, interpretation, tradition and modern application visibly distinct.**

This is a **working hypothesis**, not the final conclusion.

Further research may change it.

That is the point of doing the research.
